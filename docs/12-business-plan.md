# 12 — Business Plan & Investor Brief

**WorldView VR** | Version 1.0 | Status: Draft

## 1. Executive Summary
WorldView VR lets anyone *be anywhere* — live 360° streams from real places, AI-guided virtual tours, verified human guides, and social VR — from a headset, phone, desktop, or browser. It serves people who can't travel (disability, health, age, cost, visa, safety, time) and amplifies exploration for everyone else.

**Model:** multi-sided marketplace. Users pay for subscriptions, tours, and tips; creators earn revenue share; B2B (education, tourism boards, events, enterprise) adds high-margin contracts.

## 2. Market Sizing (TAM/SAM/SOM)

| Layer | Definition | Size (2026 est.) | Source logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TAM** | Global "virtual travel & events" spend — streaming video subscriptions (video: ~$140B), VR/AR experiences, online events, travel-tech, education field-trips | **$150–250B** | Displacement of travel ($8.5T pre-covid) is bounded by experience spend; conservatively 2–3% | 
| **SAM** | Addressable: virtual tourism + immersive media + online events platforms | **$12–20B** | VR/AR experiences (~$8B), virtual tourism/events (~$5B), immersive education (~$3B) |
| **SOM** | Realistic 5-yr capture | **$150–400M ARR** | 1.5–3M paying subs at $9/mo blended + B2B + creator GMV cuts |

Assumptions: 10M MAU by year 3, 3% blended paying, $9–12 ARPU blend. SOM is aggressive but defensible given white-space positioning.

## 3. Competitor Analysis

| Player | Strength | Weakness vs WorldView |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Earth / Street View** | Ubiquity, mapping data | Static, no liveness, no guides, low immersion |
| **YouTube (360°/Live)** | Scale, creator base | Not VR-native, no spatial audio standard, no social presence, weak live discoverability for travel |
| **Meta Horizon / Oculus TV** | VR hardware install base | Metaverse focus (virtual worlds), light on *real-world* live travel |
| **Apple Immersive Video** | Production quality | Closed ecosystem (Vision Pro only), curated/high-cost, no UGC creator economy |
| **Matterport / tours-for-RE** | Photorealistic scans | Interior-first, no live, no narrative, enterprise pricing |
| **Airlines/DMOs' "virtual tours"** | Brand trust | Static microsites, no platform, no social/monetization |
| **AmazeVR / VR event platforms** | Live VR concerts | Entertainment-only, no geographic exploration |
| **Somnium/Decentraland (metaverses)** | Avatar worlds | Synthetic environments, not real-world travel |

**Differentiators:** liveness + real-world guides + AI guidance + social + creator monetization + cross-hardware reach. None of the incumbents combine all five.

## 4. SWOT

**Strengths:** differentiated live-real-world positioning; creator marketplace network effects; accessibility mission (defensible brand + PR); B2B/G2 revenue optionality; cross-platform reach.

**Weaknesses:** content supply cold-start; heavy media infrastructure cost; real-time latency engineering risk; brand-new category (education needed); reliance on 3rd-party VR hardware + AI vendors.

**Opportunities:** silver economy (aging populations) & disability market (~1.3B people with disabilities, ~$8T purchasing power); B2B education field-trips (US K-12 market alone ~$3B/yr on field trips); tourism boards seeking sustainable alternatives; aging-in-place wellness; medical/therapeutic applications; Olympic-scale live events.

**Threats:** Big Tech entering; store-fee economics (Apple/Google 15–30%); platform dependence (Meta/Apple HW); regulatory (DSA, GDPR, filming laws); VR adoption plateau; media cost inflation.

## 6. Unit Economics (illustrative per paying user/month)

| Item | Free user | Premium user |
|---|---|---|
| Avg watch time | 12 min/day | 28 min/day |
| Media+CDN cost | $0.35 | $0.85 |
| AI inference cost | $0.08 | $0.35 |
| Compute + storage | $0.10 | $0.22 |
| Payment processing (2.9%+$0.30) | $0.15 | $0.59 |
| **Total COGS** | **$0.68** | **$2.01** |
| Revenue | $0.12 (tips/tours share) | $9.99 |
| **Gross margin** | negative (acquisition cost) | **~80%** |

Blended gross margin target: **55–65%** (mix of free COGS + B2B high margin). Contribution margin per paying user ≈ $7.9/mo.

**LTV assumptions:** premium churn 3.5%/mo → avg life ~28 mo → LTV ≈ $225. CAC ≈ $45–60 (content-led organic + creator funnel) → **LTV:CAC ≈ 4–5:1**.

**Creator economics:** creator keeps 50% of tour revenue + 70% of fan subs + 80% of tips. Platform ~30% blended take on creator GMV. Top-100 creators drive 40% of supply (healthy concentration), nurtured via partner program.

## 7. Go-To-Market

### Phase 0 (Pre-launch, 6 mo)
- Seed 30–60 creators via paid travel-influencer partnerships + equipment loaners (3 cities: Tokyo, Paris, NYC).
- Waitlist + referral mechanics; accessibility community ambassadorship (disability influencers) for authenticity.

### Phase 1 (Launch: US + UK + EU, soft)
- Freemium; free = 720p live, 10 AI asks/day, ads-free but limited; upsell premium.
- B2G pilots with 2–3 tourism boards ("Explore Ireland from home").
- PR angle: "travel for everyone" accessibility mission.

### Phase 2 (Scale: APAC, MENA, LatAm)
- Localized content, local payment rails (Adyen), local creators; language expansion to 40.
- Enterprise/education sales motion + virtual event partnerships (major festivals).

### Phase 3 (Ecosystem)
- VR-native partnership (Meta/Oculus storefront, Apple Vision Pro curated), operator deals, OEM preinstall (phones/headsets).

## 8. Pricing Strategy
- **Freemium** anchored on liveness (unique hook), premium = unlimited AI guide + 4K + VR + offline + no pay-per-tour for catalog.
- **Geo-aware pricing** (PPP) for emerging markets (40% discount) to maximize volume.
- **Bundles:** annual save ~33%; family plan (up to 5); education multi-seat.
- **Pay-as-you-go credits** ("Travel Credits") for casual users — mitigates store-fee pressure by letting users top up on web.

## 9. Marketing Plan
- **Content-led:** creator UGC clips ("60 seconds in Tokyo") as ads; YouTube/Short/Reels distribution.
- **SEO/AEO:** destination pages ("Shibuya live cam", "Machu Picchu virtual tour") — owned SEO moat.
- **Communities:** accessibility groups, expats, VR communities, expat seniors; partnerships with hospitals/care homes (B2B2C).
- **Partnerships:** tourism boards, museums, cruise lines, senior-living operators, universities.
- **Referral/creator:** revenue share + travel credit referrals.

## 10. Investor Pitch Summary
- **Problem:** Most people can't travel; existing virtual travel is passive/stale/lonely.
- **Solution:** Live, guided, social, AI-powered virtual travel — everywhere, on any device.
- **Moat:** creator supply + destination data + AI guide quality + brand trust + B2B contracts.
- **Market:** TAM $150B+; SAM $12–20B.
- **Business model:** subscriptions + pay-per-tour + tips + B2B/G2 — blended 55–65% gross margin, LTV:CAC 4:1.
- **Traction plan:** 60 seeded creators, 3 launch cities, 2 DMO pilots, waitlist 50K pre-launch.
- **Team:** VR/media engineering + AI + platform ops (see [Roadmap](13-roadmap.md) §Team).
- **Ask (Series A path):** $8M for 18 months: ~$4.5M infra/content, $3M team top-up, $500K marketing; reach 500K MAU + 3 DMO contracts before Series B at 3M MAU.

## 11. KPIs & Reporting
- North star: meaningful exploration minutes/MAU.
- Acquisition: install→signup %, time-to-first-live, waitlist→active.
- Retention: D7/D30/D90, weekly active ratio.
- Monetization: paying conversion, ARPU, creator payout growth, B2B pipeline.
- Quality: stream start time, rebuffer %, AI benchmark scores, moderation SLA, NPS.
- Financial: gross margin, media cost/stream-hour, CAC, LTV:CAC, burn multiple.
